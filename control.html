Run Flow in the background

Tab sharing may help the Flow project tab stay responsive when you switch tabs or use another app. This is experimental; keep Chrome and this extension panel open.

  1. Click Choose Flow tab.
  2. Select only your Flow project tab in Chrome’s Chrome Tab list.
  3. Click Share. Choose the correct tab—the extension cannot verify its identity.

The selected tab’s video stream stays inside this extension. It is not recorded or uploaded, and audio is not captured. Stop sharing at any time. Sharing stops when the queue finishes.
